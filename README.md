# Studying how smart contracts break.

I'm **imsaylor**. I'm learning Web3 security research in the open — reading real code,
writing proofs of concept that actually run, and documenting everything so the work
can be checked, not just believed.

No audit track record yet, and I won't pretend otherwise. What you'll find here is
the record of building one: research notes, vulnerable contracts with working
exploits and fixes, and a journal of what I'm learning — including what I get wrong.

## How I work

- **Proof over claims.** A vulnerability is real when a test exploits it. A fix is real
  when the test passes.
- **Sources, not vibes.** Research notes cite primary sources: code, documentation,
  post-mortems, and audit reports.
- **Honest labels.** Practice reports are labeled practice. Unverified ideas stay in
  the journal, not in the reports repository.
- **Local only.** All testing happens locally, on testnets, or on targets a program
  explicitly authorizes.
